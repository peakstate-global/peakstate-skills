Do we adopt a four-day week?

The board is asked to decide whether we adopt a four-day week, and the evidence we have so far supports it.

Most companies in the UK pilot kept the four-day week

In the UK four-day week pilot, 56 of the 61 companies continued with the four-day week after the trial.1 Most organisations that tried it chose to keep it.
