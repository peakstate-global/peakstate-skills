## Final position
The sources I found don't yet support the claim that AI will take over enough middle-manager work to mean far fewer middle-manager roles. Employers advertised 42% fewer middle-management positions in October than in April 2022. But the article that reports this does not name AI as a cause, and a broader study found that AI exposure shows no sign of being related to changes in employment (C3, C5, C6, F1, F2, F9).
